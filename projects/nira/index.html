About this project

NIRA (Natural IVF Response Assistant) was built during the Ben Gurion University & Soroka Medical Campus Hackathon in 2025. It provides AI-powered natural language responses to IVF treatment queries within a healthcare-compliant framework — bridging AI engineering with 17 years of healthcare infrastructure experience.
