Item 1A. Risk Factors

Investing in our securities involves a high degree of risk. You should carefully consider the following risk factors, together with all other information in this Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q, before investing in our common stock. If any of the following risks actually occur, our business, financial condition, and results of operations could be materially and adversely affected.

Competition. The digital services market is intensely competitive, and we face increasing competition from both established technology companies and emerging startups with disruptive business models. Our competitors may have greater financial, technical, and marketing resources, and may be able to respond more quickly to new technologies or changes in customer preferences.

Data Privacy and Regulatory Compliance. We operate in multiple jurisdictions with varying data privacy regulations, including GDPR, CCPA, and emerging frameworks in Asia-Pacific markets. Compliance with evolving regulations requires significant ongoing investment, and failure to comply could result in substantial fines and reputational damage.

Client Concentration. Our three largest clients accounted for approximately 34% of total revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2024. The loss of any one of these clients, or a significant reduction in their purchasing levels, could materially impact our financial results.

Supply Chain. We depend on third-party hardware vendors and cloud infrastructure providers for critical components of our service delivery. Supply chain disruptions, including those caused by geopolitical tensions, natural disasters, or component shortages, could delay deployment timelines and negatively affect client satisfaction.

Item 2. Management’s Discussion and Analysis

Total revenues for the three months ended September 30, 2024 increased $93.5 million, or 12.4%, to $847.3 million from $753.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2023. This increase was primarily attributable to growth in our Digital Services segment, which benefited from expanded enterprise cloud migration contracts and increased demand for our AI-powered analytics platform.

Operating expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2024 were $665.7 million, an increase of 8.7% compared to $612.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2023. The increase was driven primarily by higher research and development spending as we continue to invest in artificial intelligence and machine learning capabilities, partially offset by efficiency gains in general and administrative functions.
